# Landas
From the Tagalog word that translates to path, route, or direction
## AI-Powered Career Development Roadmap Platform

Landas is an AI-powered skill development platform that creates and maintains personalized career roadmaps based on a user's role, goals, existing skills, and current industry trends. The platform continuously evolves as technologies, tools, and best practices change, helping professionals focus on learning what is most relevant at any given time.

## Problem

Many professionals want to grow their careers but struggle to determine what skills to learn, where to start, and whether their learning path remains relevant. Traditional learning roadmaps quickly become outdated, while personal development plans often lack actionable guidance and adaptation to industry changes.

## Core Concept

The platform follows a simple progression:

1. Select a target role or specialization.
2. Generate a personalized career roadmap.
3. Invest skill points into learning nodes.
4. Continuously receive updates as the field evolves.

Inspired by RPG skill trees, users can specialize in career paths such as AI Engineering, Data Engineering, or Cloud Engineering. Skills are represented as interconnected nodes organized by difficulty level, dependencies, and industry relevance.

## Key Features

### Living Skill Graph
A dynamic roadmap where skills can emerge, evolve, or become deprecated as industry standards change. The graph continuously reflects current market demands and technology trends.

### AI Research Agent
An autonomous research system that monitors industry developments, discovering new tools, frameworks, methodologies, and emerging best practices from across the web.

### Personal Knowledge Integration
Connects with notes, documents, learning materials, and knowledge bases to personalize recommendations based on the user's existing expertise and learning history.

### Difficulty-Based Progression
Each skill node is categorized by difficulty level, allowing users to progress from foundational concepts to advanced specializations through a structured learning path.

### Weekly Intelligence Digest
Automatically generates personalized newsletters highlighting:
- Emerging technologies
- Relevant industry updates
- Recommended next learning steps
- Skills becoming obsolete
- New resources worth exploring

### Team Development Planning
Supports organizations by enabling managers and individual contributors to align growth goals, identify skill gaps, track development progress, and create data-driven personal development plans.
